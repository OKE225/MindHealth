# 🧠 MindHealth

MindHealth is prototype app that supports mental health. It is created for people who care about their mental and want to improve it. The app have few key features. Users can choose how they feel at day by selecting one of five emotions. This is saved in a calendar, where they can see their moods and track streak, which shows how many days in a row they selected their mood. The home page also includes tips about mental health

The app also have a mindfulness section. Each exercise includes a list of steps that explain how to do it properly. Another important feature is a thought journal, where users can write down their feelings, thoughts, and worries. This helps with self-reflection

The app also includes a personal AI assistant. Friendly chatbot that supports mental health. It responds to your emotions and needs, and helps you deal with stress and difficult feelings. It is always available when you need support

## ⭐ Key Features

- Mental health tips
- Choose how you feel today
- Calendar that shows your mood each day
- Streak system that shows how many days in a row you logged your mood
- Mindfulness exercises with step-by-step instructions
- A thought journal where you can write your feelings, thoughts, and fears
- AI chatbot
